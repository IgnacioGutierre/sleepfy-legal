Sleepfy Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Sleepfy is a sleep tracking app made by Alejandro Gutierrez, based in Madrid, Spain. This policy explains, in plain language, what data Sleepfy uses, where it lives, and what your choices are. The short version: your sleep data stays on your device and in your own iCloud. We don't run servers that store your personal data, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

1. What data Sleepfy uses

  • Your name and preferences. The name you type during onboarding, your sleep goal, and sleep habits. Stored only on your device.
  • Microphone, during sleep sessions only. When you start a sleep session, Sleepfy listens for sounds (like movement or snoring) to estimate your sleep stages. This audio is analyzed live on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. Raw audio is not kept — it is discarded during processing, and any temporary data is deleted or overwritten within 24 hours. Only the resulting numbers (like "2h deep sleep") are saved.
  • Motion data. Your device's motion sensors help estimate sleep stages. Processed on device.
  • Health data (optional). With your permission, Sleepfy reads sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, and workouts from Apple Health, and can write your sleep sessions back to it. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties for marketing or data-mining purposes. You can revoke this access anytime in the Health app.
  • Journal entries. Notes and habits you log (like caffeine or stress) to see how they affect your sleep. Stored on your device.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe, see section 3.

2. Where your data lives

Your sleep data is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, synced to your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. That iCloud data is encrypted by Apple and tied to your Apple ID — we cannot access or read it. Sleepfy has no user accounts and no servers of its own that store your sleep data.

3. The third parties involved (there are only two)

Apple — processes payments, hosts your private iCloud sync, and provides Apple Health. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat — manages our subscriptions. When you view the paywall or subscribe, RevenueCat receives an anonymous random identifier, your purchase receipt, device type, and IP address. It never receives your name, your sleep data, or your health data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

That's the whole list — no ad networks, no data brokers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites. RevenueCat processes the purchase data above to run and analyze our subscriptions.

4. Notifications

Bedtime reminders, smart alarms, and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They don't involve any server.

5. Deleting your data

  • On your device: delete the app, and its local data goes with it.
  • In iCloud: manage or delete Sleepfy's synced data in Settings → your name → iCloud, or by contacting us.
  • Apple Health: data Sleepfy wrote to Health stays under your control in the Health app, where you can delete it.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Legal basis (EU/EEA). Where GDPR applies, we process your data to provide the app and the features you use (to perform our agreement with you), and — for optional device permissions like Apple Health and the microphone — on the basis of the consent you grant in iOS, which you can withdraw at any time.

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because your data lives on your device and your iCloud, you can exercise most of these rights directly. For anything else, email us and we'll help: sleepfyapp@gmail.com. If you're in the EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Sleepfy is age-rated 12+/13+ on the App Store (the exact rating shown depends on your country or region) and is not directed at young children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Not a medical device

Sleepfy provides wellness information to help you understand your sleep. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. For medical concerns, talk to a doctor.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Meaningful changes will be highlighted in the app.
